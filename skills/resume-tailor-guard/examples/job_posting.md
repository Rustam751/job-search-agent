# Data Engineer, Shipment Visibility

**Sablewick Freight** | Denver, CO (hybrid, 2 days a week in the office) | Full-time
Salary range: $118,000 to $142,000

*This posting is fictional. The company does not exist. It is here to show how the skill works.*

## About us

Sablewick Freight runs a shipment visibility platform. Freight brokers use it to see where their loads are across
more than 200 carriers. Carrier data arrives late and in inconsistent formats, and our customers notice when it does.

## The role

You will build and own the pipelines that turn carrier feeds into one reliable picture of every shipment. You will
work with 4 other data engineers and sit close to the analysts who use your tables.

## What you will do

- Build and maintain batch pipelines in Python and SQL, orchestrated with Apache Airflow.
- Model shipment and carrier data in our Snowflake warehouse with dbt.
- Add data quality checks so that bad loads are caught before customers see them.
- Shorten load times so that morning dashboards are ready earlier.
- Take part in the on-call rotation for the data platform.

## What we are looking for

- 3 or more years of data engineering experience.
- Strong Python and SQL.
- Experience with an orchestrator (Airflow preferred) and a cloud data warehouse.
- Experience with dbt or a similar modeling tool.
- A habit of testing data as well as code.

## Nice to have

- Streaming experience (Apache Kafka or Amazon Kinesis).
- Apache Spark.
- Kubernetes and Terraform.
- A background in logistics or freight.
